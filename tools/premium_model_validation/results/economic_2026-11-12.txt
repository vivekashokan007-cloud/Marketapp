CAPITAL: reserved at the decision, reconciled at the fill, released only on an
actual close. A filled position with no exit quote at its target stays OPEN and its
capital UNRESOLVED to session end -- it is never freed on an assumed close. The
budget is tested at each decision against what is still committed, so the PEAK can
exceed it when a fill costs more than its reservation; that count is reported.

WAIT is a DECISION on every opportunity, not an absence of one: zero rupees, zero
risk. An unfilled BUY, a missing exit and an unscorable P&L are reported as their
own outcome statuses and are never counted as WAIT. Arms are compared on the same
decision-time population, and peak concurrent risk is shown because a 5-minute
cadence with a 60-minute hold overlaps up to twelve positions per contract.
